Three disciplines, one way of thinking.

Prashamsa Bandarapu works at the intersection of cybersecurity, data science, and engineering — thinking like an attacker, reasoning like an analyst, and building like someone who has to maintain the thing at 2am.

On the security side: hardening systems, reading logs for the story they're telling, and modeling how things actually break. On the data side: turning raw, messy signals into models and dashboards people can act on. On the engineering side: the pipelines, infrastructure, and automation that keep it all running.

Currently sharpening skills across threat detection, applied machine learning, and cloud infrastructure.
