about me

I'm a student at the University of Washington studying Data Science and Cybersecurity — two fields that, on the surface, look like they pull in opposite directions. One is about finding the signal in the noise. The other is about finding the person trying to hide in it. turns out that's the same skillBoth are pattern recognition under uncertainty — just pointed at different questions.

I like problems that sit at that intersection: building models that hold up under adversarial pressure, reading logs the way other people read novels, and asking not just "what does the data say" but "can I trust the data at all."

Outside of coursework, I spend a lot of time in notebooks — the paper kind and the Jupyter kind — sketching out systems before I build them. This site is more or less what those margins look like.
