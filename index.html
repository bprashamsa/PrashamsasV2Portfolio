How I use AI

I treat AI as a research partner and thinking accelerator. It helps me move from a vague idea to a structured question, explore multiple directions, and get to a testable starting point faster. The value is not in accepting the first answer; it is in having a collaborator that helps me see the problem from more than one angle.

The rule I keep

Across security, engineering, and data science, AI helps me turn vague ideas into structured plans, explore alternatives, and identify edge cases I may not have considered. I still own the reasoning: I check the evidence, test the behavior, and decide whether the result actually makes sense. AI is a starting point for me, never the answer, especially when the work involves security, sensitive data, or real-world consequences.
