How I work:

Start with people, build something small, and let feedback decide what's next.

  1. Listen

    Before proposing anything, I find out what's actually "broken", who feels it, and what's already been tried. What people ask for and what they need are often different things.

  2. Connect

    Bring in people who see the problem differently, then look for the overlap, the blind spots, and the question nobody has asked yet.

  3. Build

    Turn the strongest ideas into small experiments, clear artifacts, and things people can actually respond to.

  4. Iterate

    Treat feedback as data. What surprises us gets tested, what holds up gets kept, and the people closest to the problem stay in the room.

How I use AI

I treat AI as a research partner and thinking accelerator. It helps me move from a vague idea to a structured question, explore multiple directions, and get to a testable starting point faster. The value is not in accepting the first answer; it is in having a collaborator that helps me see the problem from more than one angle.
